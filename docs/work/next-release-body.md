- **Marketplace through HA NOVA (Home Assistant 2026.11+).** HA NOVA now installs, updates, and uninstalls Marketplace items, each after your own confirmation. With the NOVA App, if Home Assistant still needs its Marketplace risk notice accepted, HA NOVA first asks you, as a step of its own, to accept it for the App's Home Assistant user: the Supervisor system user, which every App with access to Home Assistant shares. Home Assistant offers no way to withdraw that. A standalone Relay can't do this step: sign in to Home Assistant as HA NOVA's user and accept it under Settings → Marketplace (#1099).
- **Every Home Assistant administrator can use the NOVA page.** Not only the owner: any administrator can now open NOVA in the sidebar, make a code for their computer, revoke a computer, and approve. Turning chat apps on or off and their settings stay with the owner. (README at release prep: the "Waiting for your OK" line about other administrators finding a link, and the lines where only the owner makes the six-digit code or clicks Connect a device — today around lines 41, 148, and 156 — change with this.)
- **Which integration is failing, without reading the log.** HA NOVA now reads log facts: for each integration of your home, how many log entries of each level (errors, warnings, and so on) Home Assistant's log still holds and since when. It never reads the log's lines, which can hold passwords and addresses; for those it still asks you to look under Settings → System → Logs. Needs HA NOVA 0.31 and the NOVA App of HA NOVA 0.31.
- **A system check-up: is my Home Assistant OK, why is it slow, would my backup work?** One question gets one answer computed by HA NOVA, not guessed by the AI: Supervisor health and its issues, free space, memory and CPU of Home Assistant and your running Apps, integrations that fail to load, open repairs, the newest backup (age, what's in it, where its copies are, the automatic schedule), and pending updates. It never changes anything, says what it could not read, and never claims a backup restores. Memory and CPU need the NOVA App of HA NOVA 0.31.
- **Chat apps on the standalone Relay container ask the phone linked to it.** In 0.30, a chat app on the container refused every change that needs an OK. Now any chat user's unlock, delete, or file change asks the one phone linked to the container, and the chat waits for it as for your own phone; with no phone linked there, it's still refused and nothing is sent. Everyone using the container asks as one: a new ask cancels the open one, and anyone allowed such changes can pause these confirmations for 5 minutes by letting five asks in a row go unanswered; nothing is approved or sent then (`docs/reference/chat-apps.md`). The container needs no update.

## Fixed in 0.31

- **Fewer phone asks in homes with a UI thermostat.** On a NOVA App with the safety check, a thermostat or hygrostat helper you made in Home Assistant's UI, in a home where an automation reacts to service calls, made everyday changes such as a light wait for your OK on the phone. The App now reads which switch the helper turns on, as HA NOVA on your computer already did, so those changes run without an ask again. Changes that need your OK still ask.

## Changed in 0.31

- **Phone asks pause less often, and you can end a pause.** A computer's phone asks now pause only after five of its asks in a row go unanswered, for 5 minutes (before: three, unanswered or cancelled, for 15 minutes). Declining never counts, and neither does cancelling within 15 seconds. The NOVA page shows which requests went unanswered and when, and "Ask on the phone again" ends the pause at once.
- **A clearer NOVA page that updates on its own.** A request waiting for your OK now sits at the top with a "Confirm or decline…" button, and appears and disappears without reloading the page. The approval page shows each request once, in large type, from which computer, with a live countdown and side-by-side Approve and Cancel buttons, and takes you back to the NOVA page once nothing is left to answer. Status, computers, and chat apps are each one short card; details such as the ChatGPT steps and what each level allows open on a click, and the chat-app address has a Copy button.

- **Fixing a repair and changing an integration's sub-entries now ask for your OK.** What such a fix or change does is up to the integration (it may remove devices or entities), so HA NOVA asks before each step, on every route, also when an AI app sends the request directly. Renaming a device's entity onto the id of one it replaces, and moving an entity, device, or room into an area, floor, or label, ask too when an automation triggered there does something that needs your OK, such as unlocking.
