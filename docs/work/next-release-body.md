## Upgrade notes for 0.31

- **Update the NOVA App together with HA NOVA.** Log facts, the check-up's memory and CPU numbers, the NOVA page for every administrator, the new NOVA page and approval page, the window's "Open in Home Assistant" button, the shorter phone-ask pause, the UI thermostat fix, and the fix for large homes' entity lists need the NOVA App of HA NOVA 0.31. With an older App, HA NOVA 0.31 works as 0.30 did for these.
- **Using HA NOVA in ChatGPT:** after the App update, open chatgpt.com/plugins, choose HA NOVA, then Refresh.

## New Features

- **Marketplace through HA NOVA (Home Assistant 2026.11+).** HA NOVA now installs, updates, and uninstalls Marketplace items, each after your own confirmation. With the NOVA App, if Home Assistant still needs its Marketplace risk notice accepted, HA NOVA first asks you, as a step of its own, to accept it for the App's Home Assistant user: the Supervisor system user, which every App with access to Home Assistant shares. Home Assistant offers no way to withdraw that. A standalone Relay can't do this step: sign in to Home Assistant as HA NOVA's user and accept it under Settings → Marketplace (#1099).
- **"Can I update now?" gets a checked answer.** Before an update, HA NOVA checks whether your home is ready (Supervisor health, free space, a running update or backup, a recent backup that includes Home Assistant, open repairs), suggests an order (a backup first, the NOVA App last, an App that needs the new Home Assistant after it), and matches the backward-incompatible changes in Home Assistant's release notes to the integrations you use, with the automations and scripts that use them. After the update it says what was installed, what rolled back, and which integrations, entities, or automations newly fail. It only reads; a check it couldn't run is named, never reported as fine.
- **A system check-up: is my Home Assistant OK, why is it slow, would my backup work?** One question gets one answer computed by HA NOVA, not guessed by the AI: Supervisor health and its issues, free space, memory and CPU of Home Assistant and your running Apps, integrations that fail to load, open repairs, the newest backup (age, what's in it, where its copies are, the automatic schedule), and pending updates. It never changes anything, says what it could not read, and never claims a backup restores. Memory and CPU need the NOVA App of HA NOVA 0.31.
- **Which integration is failing, without reading the log.** HA NOVA now reads log facts: for each integration of your home, how many log entries of each level (errors, warnings, and so on) Home Assistant's log still holds and since when. It never reads the log's lines, which can hold passwords and addresses; for those it still asks you to look under Settings → System → Logs. Needs HA NOVA 0.31 and the NOVA App of HA NOVA 0.31.
- **Every Home Assistant administrator can use the NOVA page.** Not only the owner: any administrator can now open NOVA in the sidebar, make a code for their computer, revoke a computer, and approve. Turning chat apps on or off and their settings stay with the owner.
- **A clearer NOVA page that updates on its own.** A request waiting for your OK now sits at the top with a "Confirm or decline…" button, and appears and disappears without reloading the page. The approval page shows each request once, in large type, from which computer, with a live countdown and side-by-side Approve and Cancel buttons, and takes you back to the NOVA page once nothing is left to answer. Status, computers, and chat apps are each one short card; details such as the ChatGPT steps and what each level allows open on a click, and the chat-app address has a Copy button.
- **The confirmation window on your computer opens the request in Home Assistant.** With the NOVA App's safety check, the window on your Mac, Windows, or Linux computer can't confirm a change itself. Its "Open in Home Assistant" button now opens exactly this request on the NOVA approval page in your browser, through Home Assistant Link or your local address, whichever HA NOVA uses; you press Approve there (or confirm on your phone). The window says so. Needs the NOVA App of HA NOVA 0.31; with an older App the button opens the NOVA page as before.
- **Your phone asks sooner.** An unlock or another change that needs your OK now rings your phone before HA NOVA's full check of your home, which on a large home over Home Assistant Link took 13 to 15 seconds; the full check runs right after your OK, and nothing is sent unless both pass on exactly the same request. Planning an unlock also reads less of your home.
- **Chat apps on the standalone Relay container ask the phone linked to it.** In 0.30, a chat app on the container refused every change that needs an OK. Now any chat user's unlock, delete, or file change asks the one phone linked to the container, and the chat waits for it as for your own phone; with no phone linked there, it's still refused and nothing is sent. Everyone using the container asks as one: a new ask cancels the open one, and anyone allowed such changes can pause these confirmations for 5 minutes by letting five asks in a row go unanswered; nothing is approved or sent then (`docs/reference/chat-apps.md`). The container needs no update.
- **In chat apps, reading your home no longer waits for a change in progress.** Finding, reading, history, and traces answer while a change runs, instead of answering "busy" or queuing behind it (#919). Changes still run one at a time.
- **New automations use Home Assistant's newer room, floor, and label triggers.** HA NOVA writes and reviews the purpose-specific triggers and conditions the automation editor has offered since Home Assistant 2026.7, with targets and exclusions, instead of generic state triggers.
- **A helper made through its setup form is checked by HA NOVA itself.** After the create, HA NOVA confirms the helper is loaded and lists its entities and settings, instead of relying on the AI to read them back.

## What To Watch

- **Phone asks pause less often, and you can end a pause.** A computer's phone asks now pause only after five of its asks in a row go unanswered, for 5 minutes (before: three, unanswered or cancelled, for 15 minutes). Declining never counts, and neither does cancelling within 15 seconds. The NOVA page shows which requests went unanswered and when, and "Ask on the phone again" ends the pause at once.
- **More changes ask when an automation could react to them.** Renaming a device's entity onto the id of one it replaces, and moving an entity, device, or room into an area, floor, or label, ask when an automation that would then react does something that needs your OK, such as unlocking. Steps HA NOVA's instructions mark as needing your OK now ask on every route, also when an AI app sends the request directly.

## Bug Fixes

- **Fewer phone asks in homes with a UI thermostat.** On a NOVA App with the safety check, a thermostat or hygrostat helper you made in Home Assistant's UI, in a home where an automation reacts to service calls, made everyday changes such as a light wait for your OK on the phone. The App now reads which switch the helper turns on, as HA NOVA on your computer already did, so those changes run without an ask again. Changes that need your OK still ask.
- **Large homes: everyday changes no longer ask on the phone because the entity list failed.** On a NOVA App with the safety check, a home whose full entity list is several MB (thousands of entities) got "HTTP 500" when HA NOVA read it. HA NOVA then couldn't tell which automations react to a change, so it asked for your OK on everyday changes such as a light. The App now passes the full list. Changes that need your OK still ask.
- **The guided NOVA App update no longer refuses in homes with automations that react to service calls.** `ha-nova update` offered the App update and then refused without a reason. It now checks first and asks for your confirmation like any change that needs your OK.
- **After `ha-nova update`, the NOVA App check no longer hangs.** It shows a progress line and gives up after 10 seconds with a pointer to `ha-nova doctor`; it never offers an update it couldn't check. Checking for updates also runs the App check alongside the release lookup.
- **Windows: `ha-nova update` retries a briefly locked install folder.** Before, a busy computer could make the update fail with "access denied"; it failed safe, and running it again worked.
- **Chat apps: a phone OK checks the user's role again right before the change.** An administrator demoted while their phone's OK waited for another change can no longer apply a change their new role doesn't allow.
